A note on accuracy

Where the lines fall is real astronomy and is calculated precisely. What they mean comes from astrological tradition, mainly Jim Lewis's system from the 1970s, not from scientific research. Treat the readings as something to reflect on, not a forecast.

How to read the lines

4 lines per planet

At the moment you were born, every planet was rising somewhere on Earth, setting somewhere else, overhead in one place and underfoot in another. Each of those spots becomes a line. The planet sets the theme; the line says where in your life it shows up.

Distance. A line is usually said to be strongest within about 300 km and to fade by around 1,000 km. There's no agreed cutoff.

Crossings. Where two lines cross, astrologers read both meanings together. The ring on the map marks the city you're checking.
